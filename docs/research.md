# Why this rebuild

Chosen 20 October 2026 as the first rebuild in the employee relations family.

- **The bill.** Salesforce publishes Agentforce HR Service pricing: AU$21 per employee per month for AI Agents for Employees, AU$70 per HR user per month for the HR Service Desk Enterprise Edition and AU$105 for Unlimited, billed annually, with Agentforce for Service at AU$175 per user per month as an add-on (https://www.salesforce.com/service/hr-service-management/pricing/, checked 20 October 2026). The employee licence is charged on every employee, so the bill grows with headcount, not with the size of the HR team. The specialist case tools it competes with (HR Acuity, Case IQ, Elker) quote privately.
- **Data-shaped.** People, cases, allegations, process steps, interviews, evidence, decisions and warnings, and weekly rituals: answer the requests before their deadlines, move investigations along, check the process before a warning, chase what has gone quiet.
- **The rules are real and checkable.** New Zealand's Employment Relations Act 2000 (flexible work in one month, statements of reasons in 14 days, the s 103A fair process test, the 90-day and 12-month grievance windows), the Privacy Act 2020 (20 working days, with the summer close-down), the Protected Disclosures Act 2022, and Australia's Fair Work Act 2009 (21-day responses under s 65A and s 76A, the s 387 factors, the 21-day dismissal window).
- **What stays with the custom build.** The employee portal and chat, knowledge articles, and Slack or Teams notifications.
